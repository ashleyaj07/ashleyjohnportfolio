A J

a bit of everything, on purpose.

I studied IT Innovation & Design at Conestoga College, spent the last two years learning how to keep calm behind a Tim Hortons counter when there are definitely someone screaming at you for their cappuccino , and used up every golden hour making videos for the sake of good lighting to build a reading community online. I'm now settling into Toronto and figuring out how those three things — technical training, service-industry patience, and a genuine love of stories and content — turn into a career.

I'm drawn to the parts of media that happen off-camera: the caption that took four drafts, the edit that makes a video actually land, the little UX decision that makes an app feel right. That's the work I want to keep doing.
